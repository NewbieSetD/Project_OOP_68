# Game Concept: Larp Pig// fix soon 
- Core Idea: Easy to understand and play, yet demands strategic decision-making and delivers continuous tension.

- Game Description:
Players pilot a tank with the simple goal of destroying enemies and surviving. High stakes define the gameplay: tanks are extremely fragile and die from a single hit. Players start with a limited supply of only 4 rounds of ammunition, but can retrieve additional ammo from destroyed enemy wrecks up to a maximum capacity of 6 rounds.

The game features a top-down perspective with a retro 90s arcade-style aesthetic.

- Controls & Mechanics
Players maneuver their tank using either W, A, S, D or the Arrow Keys. Movement is restricted to 4 cardinal directions (no diagonal movement or smooth turning):

W / Up Arrow: Move forward in the direction the turret/cannon is pointing.

S / Down Arrow: Reverse away from the facing direction.

A / Left Arrow: Rotate the tank 90 degrees counter-clockwise (can rotate full 360 degrees).

D / Right Arrow: Rotate the tank 90 degrees clockwise (can rotate full 360 degrees).

Firing: Players can shoot by left-clicking anywhere on the game screen or pressing the Spacebar. Aiming is fixed,bullets strictly fire directly along the direction the tank's turret is facing.

- Basic Rules
Looting Ammo: Defeated enemies drop 1 round of ammunition on death, which players must physically drive over to collect.

Fail-Safe Softlock Protection: If a player intentionally wastes all ammo or encounters an unwinnable softlock state, a popup prompt will appear asking to restart the match.

Collision Mechanics: Bullets do not pierce tanks. They detonate immediately upon impact with any entity, wall, or object.

Game Modes
Offline Mode
Bot Survival: Fight endless waves of AI tanks spawning randomly throughout the battlefield. Your score is based on Total Kills and Survival Time.

Online Modes (PvP Free-For-All)
No teams,every player for themselves. Players instantly respawn at randomized spawn points until the match condition is met.

Timed Deathmatch: The player with the highest kill count at the end of 2:30 minutes wins.

Killstreak Challenge: The first player to reach a 5-kill streak without dying wins the match.
